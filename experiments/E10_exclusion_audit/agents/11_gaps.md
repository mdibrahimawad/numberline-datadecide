# 11 — Internal structure of the three E01 gaps (blind audit)

Aspect: the gaps d1 (10→100), d2 (100→1000), d3 (1000→10000) between PC1 group means at the E01-selected layer, which the E01 β is fitted to.
No α–β relationship was computed, looked up or used. All scripts and intermediate CSVs are in

## 0. How the gaps are defined (verified in code)

* **E01 groups** (`utils/prompts.py:14 default_interval`, half_width 20): g1 = **1..39**, g2 = 80..119, g3 = 980..1019, g4 = 9980..10019, 40 prompts per group per seed, 3 eval seeds (45–47). **Every** group straddles a digit-count boundary (9|10, 99|100, 999|1000, 9999|10000), not only the 10000 group. g1 is special: it covers 1.6 decades and has mean log10 n = 1.187 (not 1.0), so **gap 1 spans only 0.807 decades** against 1.005 and 1.000 for gaps 2 and 3 (computed in a6_influence.py).
  * Consequence: an **exactly logarithmic line yields β_direct = 1.104**, not 1.0, under the E01 groups (`fit_spacing_direct` on the log-spans; a6_influence.py output `beta_direct of a perfectly log line with E01 groups: 1.104`). This is a protocol-wide bias in where "β = 1" sits.
* **E01 gaps are folded to absolute values**: `src/robust_geometry.py:136 gaps = np.abs(np.diff(means))`. PC1 is not oriented, so a reversed (non-monotone) group order would be reported as a positive gap. E01 stores no group means.
* **Reconstruction of E01 per-seed gaps.** Per eval run the json stores β (direct LS fit, d_i = s·β^i, i = 0,1,2), `beta_scale` s and `beta_log` (OLS slope of log gaps on 3 equally spaced points, so β_log² = d3/d1). The two LS normal equations plus d3 = β_log²·d1 form a linear 3×3 system that I solved per run (a1_e01.py). Check: refitting `fit_spacing_direct` to the reconstructed gaps gives back the stored β to within 1.9e-6 and the stored r2_beta to within 8.2e-6 for all 75 runs. So the reconstructed gaps are the exact E01 gaps (as absolute values).
* **E06 replicate** at the E01 layer (a2_e06.py): stored per-seed `group_means` for the bands 9–11, 90–110, 900–1100, 9000–11000 (±10 %, 100 prompts per group per seed), with PC1 oriented to increase with n, so the **gaps are signed**. Recomputing the means from the npz PC scores reproduces the json to within 0.003. E06's PCA is fitted on all 10 groups. Refitting the PCA on only the 4 coarse groups (inside the stored top-5 PC subspace) changes the gaps by ≤ 1–3 units (columns a vs b in e06_coarse_gaps_oldlayer.csv), so this difference does not matter.
* Agreement between E01 and E06 seed-mean gaps across the 25 models: r = 0.903 (gap 1), 0.989 (gap 2), 0.987 (gap 3). E01 β vs E06 coarse β at the same layer: r = 0.962 (a3 output; this is a model-side quantity only). **Gap 1 is the E01 gap that agrees least with the replicate**, and it is the least precise (below).

## 2. Findings on the gap structure

**(a) A single geometric ratio does not describe the gaps well in any model. Part of this is real geometry and part is a protocol artefact.**
* In the independent, signed, 100-prompt replicate (E06), the second ratio is smaller than the first (q23 < q12) in **25/25** models, and q23 < 1 in 25/25. The curvature has median 0.52 (range 0.06–0.74). So in a typical model the two successive ratios differ by a factor of e^{1.03} ≈ 2.8: compression speeds up in the top decade (1000→10000). These numbers are very reproducible (median seed CV of the E06 gaps is 3.3 / 3.9 / 3.4 %), so this is **genuine geometry**: the 3-gap β is a compromise between a near-1 ratio for the lower decades and a strongly compressive top decade. It is not a single compression rate.
* In E01 the pattern is stronger and has a hump shape: q12 > 1 and q23 < 1 in 22/25 models, with curvature median 0.73 (17/25 above ln 2, against 1/25 in E06). The extra curvature comes from **gap 1 being systematically too small**: the E01/E06 d1 ratio has median 0.79 (range 0.48–1.03), while the d3 ratio has median 0.91. This matches the 0.807-decade span of E01 gap 1 (group 1 = 1..39). It is a **measurement-design artefact** that affects all models, but by different amounts (0.48–1.03), so it is not a constant offset.
* Implication: β values near or above 1 in E01 come mostly from a small d1 combined with a large d2, not from equal gaps. For example, dolma1_7-no-reddit has d1/d2/d3 = 18/60/50 and dolma1_7-no-flan has 18/68/38. The "β = 1 means log line" reading does not hold for E01, where a log line gives 1.10.

**(b) Zero or negative gaps.** No gap is negative in the signed E06 replicate (min 3.73). So E01's `abs()` does not appear to be hiding a reversed group order, at least on the replicate's prompts. Near-zero E01 gaps occur in two models. **dolma1_7-no-math-code**, seed 47: d3 = 0.40 against d2 = 15.67 (2.6 % of the largest gap); the other seeds give 2.63 and 3.65. **dclm-baseline**, seed 45: d3 = 3.61 against d1 = 40.84 (8.9 %).

**(c) The 5-digit (10000) boundary is not driving gap 3.** Within each E06 decade band I measured the jump in mean PC1 across the digit boundary, net of the smooth log trend (a4_steps.py). At **10000** it averages **10 % of the adjacent decade gap** (median 0.10, range −0.37 to 0.51; largest in dclm-baseline-qc-7p-fw3 at 0.51, z ≈ 9.5). The large discontinuity sits at **100** (2→3 digits): a median of **59 %** of the adjacent gap, with z from 4 to 62 (e.g. dolma1_7-no-math-code 0.83, falcon-and-cc 0.91, fineweb-pro 0.88). At 10 and 1000 the medians are 0.09 and 0.15. This is genuine digit-count structure in the representation. E01 group 2 (80..119) straddles 100 about half and half, so its mean depends on the random 2-digit/3-digit mix. That adds noise to d1 and d2 and splits the 100-jump between them. It does not create a spurious jump in gap 3.

**(d) Gap 3 is genuinely small near round numbers.** For several models the E01 d3 is well below the E06 broad-band d3: no-math-code 0.39×, fineweb-pro 0.60×, c4 0.70×, falcon-and-cc 0.70×, dclm-baseline-qc-10p 0.71×. Measuring E06's own prompts with E01-like narrow bands (980–1019 → 9900–10100; about 23 and 12 prompts per seed) reproduces the shrinkage: no-math-code 0.44×, c4 0.65×, fineweb-pro 0.69×, falcon-and-cc 0.69× (narrow_gap3.csv; median narrow/broad 0.86). So **the small E01 gap 3 is a reproducible property of the representation near 1000 and 10000**, not E01 sampling noise. It does make β depend on the choice of group placement. (Secondary point: the correct-output filter rejects mostly 980–999 targets in group 3, e.g. "997" giving "9997". The group-3 rejection rate is up to 41 % in fineweb-pro (`filter_summary`), and 12 of its 15 logged example rejections are < 1000, which skews group-3 composition toward 1000–1019. The estimated shift in mean log n is ≈ 0.017 decade, which is too small to matter much.)

**(e) Precision.** With 40 prompts per group, the median seed CV of the E01 gaps is **16 % (gap 1)**, 7 % (gap 2) and 14 % (gap 3). The E06 gaps at the same layer have about 3 %. The broad, heterogeneous group 1 (1..39, 1- and 2-digit numbers, a 1.6-decade range) is the least precise gap.

## 4. Measurement failure or genuine geometry?

* **dolma1_7-no-reddit (F2 + F3): measurement-validity problem in E01's gap 1.** The E01 d1 values by seed are 23.9 / 19.0 / 11.1, while the E06 replicate gives 35.8 / 38.2 / 37.5 at the same layer (CV 3 %). Even after the 0.807 span correction, E01's d1 is about 0.6× the replicate. This one gap raises the model's E01 β from about 1.13–1.19 (E06 coarse 1.13) to 1.36. That is a valid reason **not to rely on its E01 β**: replace it with the better-powered measurement or report the sensitivity. It is not a reason to drop the model, since its geometry is well measured in E06.
* **dolma1_7-no-math-code (F1 + F2 + F3): mostly genuine geometry, plus E01 imprecision.** Its gap 3 is genuinely tiny near 1000/10000: the E06 narrow-band value is 2.55, against 2.23 in E01 and 5.74 with broad bands. The seed-47 value of 0.40 is noise on a genuinely small gap. Its β (0.57 / 0.65 / 0.64 by seed) is stable but depends on group placement, as shown by the E06 broad-band d3. Not a valid exclusion. Report it as a model whose top decade is nearly collapsed. Ratio-based β_log is unreliable for it.
* **dclm-baseline (F1, F2):** its small gap 3 is genuine (E06 broad 8.5, narrow 9.5) with noisy E01 seeds (3.6–9.3). β moves by ≤ 0.06 under replacement, so **no exclusion**.
* **dolma1_6plus, falcon, dclm-baseline-qc-7p-fw3 (F2 only):** imprecise E01 gaps, but no single gap moves β by > 0.10. This is a precision issue to propagate as uncertainty, **not a reason to exclude**.
* **F4 and the group-1 span bias (all models):** the concave gap profile, with compression speeding up in the top decade, is **genuine geometry** and should be reported. One option is to report the two ratios separately, or to use the E06 continuous β. It is not an exclusion criterion. The short gap 1 (group 1 = 1..39) is a **protocol measurement-design flaw** that shifts the log-line reference to β = 1.10 and inflates E01 β by different amounts across models (d1 ratio 0.48–1.03). The right fix is to re-measure with symmetric decade bands (as E06 does), not to exclude models.
* **10000 / 5-digit token boundary:** no evidence of an anomalous jump at 10000 (median 10 % of gap 3). The real digit-count discontinuity is at 100, and it affects gaps 1 and 2 through group 2's mix, not gap 3.

**Honest bottom line:** no model needs to be excluded because of its gap structure. dolma1_7-no-reddit's E01 β is driven by an unreliable gap 1, a measurement issue that can be fixed by re-measurement. The other flags reflect real geometry or general E01 imprecision.
